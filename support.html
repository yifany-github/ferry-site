开始使用

第一次打开时，选好你的母语和英语水平，然后点“先读两本英语样书”就能马上体验。之后可以在“设置 › 帮助 › 使用指南”里随时重看。

常见问题

能导入哪些格式？

EPUB、MOBI/AZW3、FB2、Word（.docx）、ODT、RTF、TXT、Markdown、HTML、带文字的 PDF，以及带字幕的音频和视频。在书库点“＋”从“文件”导入；也可以在 Safari 等 App 里点“分享 › 保存到渡读”，把网页文章直接存进书库。扫描版 PDF（图片）无法查词。带 DRM 加密的电子书无法导入。

为什么有的词上方有释义，有的没有？

渡读会根据你的英语水平，给超出水平的词在原文上方标出简短释义。可以在“设置 › 查词与学习 › 英语水平”里调整水平；在“设置 › 阅读排版”里可以关闭“按英语水平标出生词”。你自己存进生词本的词也会标出来，标为“已掌握”后就不再显示。

查词需要联网吗？

不需要。轻点查词使用内置的离线英汉词典，飞行模式下也能用。

AI 翻译和讲解怎么开启？

这两项是可选功能，使用 Google Gemini，需要你自己的 Gemini API Key：

  1. 用 Google 账号打开 Google AI Studio，创建一个 API Key。
  2. 在渡读里打开“设置 › AI 服务”，粘贴这个 Key。

Key 只保存在本机钥匙串里，所选文字由你的设备直接发给 Google。费用和用量由 Google 按你的账号计算，Gemini 有免费额度。部分国家和地区无法使用 Gemini API。详见隐私政策。

AI 请求失败，或提示设置 AI 服务？

请检查 Key 是否完整粘贴、是否已在 Google AI Studio 中被删除，以及当前网络能否访问 Google。离线词典、朗读和其他功能不受影响。

换手机后书和生词还在吗？

渡读没有账号，内容只保存在本机。换机前请在“设置 › 学习记录与备份”导出备份，再在新设备上导入。用 iCloud 或电脑整机备份恢复的设备，通常也会一起恢复渡读的数据。

换了设备，需要再买一次吗？

不需要。用同一个 Apple ID 登录 App Store，在“已购项目”里可以免费重新下载渡读。家人共享开启后，家庭成员也可以下载。

怎么申请退款？

付款由 Apple 处理，请在 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请退款。

Reader Pro 是什么？

设置里的 Reader Pro 是以后推出的订阅服务，目前标注为“即将推出”，不能购买。现在用自己的 Gemini Key 就能使用全部 AI 功能。

Support (English)

On first launch, choose your native language and English level, then tap “Start with Two English Samples” to try it right away. You can reopen the guide any time in Settings › Help › Guide.

Which formats can I import?

EPUB, MOBI/AZW3, FB2, Word (.docx), ODT, RTF, TXT, Markdown, HTML, PDFs with real text, and audio or video with subtitles. Tap “+” in the library to import from Files, or use Share › Save to Ferry in Safari and other apps. Scanned (image-only) PDFs and DRM-protected books can’t be used.

Why do some words have a gloss above them?

Ferry glosses words above your English level. Change your level in Settings › Lookup & Learning › English Level, or turn off “Gloss Words Above My Level” in Settings › Reading Layout. Words you save are marked too, until you mark them as mastered.

Does the dictionary need a connection?

No. Tap-to-look-up uses a built-in offline dictionary and works in airplane mode.

How do I turn on AI translation and explanations?

They’re optional and use Google Gemini with your own API key:

  1. Sign in to Google AI Studio with a Google account and create an API key.
  2. In Ferry, open Settings › AI Service and paste the key.

The key stays in your device’s Keychain, and text is sent from your device directly to Google. Google bills usage to your account; Gemini has a free tier. The Gemini API isn’t available in every country or region. See the privacy policy.

AI requests fail, or it asks you to set up the AI service?

Check that the whole key was pasted, that it still exists in Google AI Studio, and that your network can reach Google. The offline dictionary, reading aloud and everything else keep working.

Will my books and words move to a new phone?

Ferry has no accounts, so everything is stored on the device. Before switching, export a backup in Settings › Study Records & Backup and import it on the new device. Restoring a whole-device iCloud or computer backup usually brings Ferry’s data along.

Do I need to buy it again on a new device?

No. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID and download Ferry again from Purchased. With Family Sharing on, family members can download it too.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payment. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What is Reader Pro?

Reader Pro is a future subscription and is marked “Coming soon” in Settings; it can’t be purchased yet. Every AI feature works today with your own Gemini key.
